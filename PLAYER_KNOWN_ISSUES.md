# Known Issues

Plain-language list of things that are not finished or not fully checked yet.

## Needs approval before release

**CONF-001 — QuickPlay deck lists are our proposal, not printed lists.**
The uploaded sources describe 30-card standard decks. QuickPlay needs 20 cards
(ten titles, two copies each), so each deck is a ten-title cut we selected from the
approved source cards. No card name, stat, keyword, or rules text was changed. These
cuts still need sign-off before the game can be called final.

## Not yet verified

**Offline play after installing the app.**
Everything needed for offline is in place, but we have not confirmed a full offline
reload on a real installed build. If you install the app and lose connection on the
very first launch, it may not open until it has loaded online once.

## Minor

**Art pending on 21 non-card records.**
Gates, bosses, and a few reference records still show placeholder frames. All 153
playable QuickPlay cards have final art.

**"Lantern Array" resolves once, on arrival.**
QuickPlay has no step for using an ability later in the turn, so this card applies
its effect the moment it enters play instead of repeatedly.

**Undo is limited.**
Undo is offered only where it cannot reveal hidden information. After a draw, a
reveal, or a handoff, the action stands.

**Cloud accounts are not enabled.**
All progress, settings, and saved matches live on the device you play on. Use
Settings > Data Management to export a backup before clearing browser data.
